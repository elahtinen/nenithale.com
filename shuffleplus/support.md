# Shuffle+ Support

Shuffle+ shuffles the music in your Apple Music library and remembers your place in each shuffle, so you can switch between them and pick up where you left off.

## Contact

Email [support@nenithale.com](mailto:support@nenithale.com). Please say what happened and what you expected, and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

If the problem is with playback, a diagnostics report helps. In Shuffle+, open Settings, tap **Share Diagnostics**, and send the report to the address above. It contains recent playback events and version details. It does not contain song titles, playlist names or account details.

## Common questions

### Do I need an Apple Music subscription?

No. Shuffle+ plays the music in your Apple Music library. Songs you bought or synced from a computer play without a subscription. Songs you added from the Apple Music catalog need an active Apple Music subscription, as they do in the Music app.

### Shuffle+ shows no music, or asks for access

Shuffle+ needs permission to read your music library. Open the Settings app, find Shuffle+ in the list of apps, and turn on **Media & Apple Music**.

### What is a saved shuffle?

When you start a shuffle, Shuffle+ keeps its song order and your place in it. Come back later, even after playing something else, and it resumes at the same song and time.

### What happens when a shuffle reaches the end?

It repeats. After the last song, the shuffle starts again from the first song in the same order. To get a new order, swipe left on the playlist in the list and tap **Reshuffle**.

### Will I hear the same artist twice in a row?

Shuffle+ does its best to prevent it. Each new shuffle is arranged so that songs by the same artist are kept apart. When one artist makes up more than half of a playlist, some back-to-back songs can't be avoided, and Shuffle+ keeps those to a minimum.

### What do I get for free, and what is Unlimited?

The free plan keeps three saved shuffles: your Library, which is always included, and two playlists. Unlimited is a one-time purchase that lets you keep a saved shuffle for every playlist. It adds nothing else, and it does not include Apple Music. CarPlay works on both plans.

### My two free spots are full

Forget a saved shuffle to free its spot: swipe left on the playlist in the list and tap **Forget**. Or unlock Unlimited.

### I bought Unlimited, but the app shows the free plan

Open Settings in Shuffle+ and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure the iPhone is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase, or to a Family Sharing member of it. Restoring also brings Unlimited back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling.

### How do I get a refund?

Apple handles App Store refunds. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### Are my shuffles synced to my other devices?

No. Your shuffle orders and saved places stay on the iPhone where you made them.

### Does force-quitting the app stop the music?

Yes. Open Shuffle+ again and tap play to resume from your saved place.

### What happens when I change a playlist in Apple Music?

Shuffle+ keeps the order and your place for the songs that remain, and adds new songs to the end of the shuffle.

### I added songs, but they haven't come up in the shuffle

New songs join at the end of the saved shuffle, so they play after the songs that were already waiting. They stay there each time the shuffle repeats. To mix them in, swipe left on the playlist in the list and tap **Reshuffle**, which starts a new order from the beginning.

# Shuffle+ Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Shuffle+ (listed on the App Store as Shuffle Plus) is an iPhone app published by Nenithale.

**Shuffle+ does not collect your data.** There is no Shuffle+ account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## Your music library

With your permission, Shuffle+ reads the playlists and songs in your Apple Music library so that it can play them. This happens on your iPhone and nothing is sent to us. You can withdraw the permission at any time in the Settings app.

## What stays on your iPhone

Your shuffle orders, your saved places, your preferences and a diagnostic log are stored on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. We cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them.

## Apple services

Shuffle+ has no servers of its own. It connects only to Apple, for Apple Music and for App Store purchases. Apple processes any payment, and we never receive your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. Apple's privacy policy is at [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Diagnostics and support

The Share Diagnostics button in Settings prepares a report of recent playback events and version details, with no song titles, playlist names or account details. It leaves your iPhone only if you choose to send it.

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

# Shuffle+

**Shuffle. Switch. Resume.**

Shuffle+ is an iPhone app that shuffles the music in your Apple Music library. Each playlist keeps its own shuffle order and its own place, so you can switch between them and pick up where you left off. It works with CarPlay.
